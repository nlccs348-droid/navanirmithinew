About Us — Who We Are

Navanirmithi Labour Contract Co-operative Society Ltd. No. PT 348, based in Pathanamthitta, Kerala, is a professionally managed co-operative society dedicated to delivering high-quality services in construction, electrical works, cleaning, quick commerce, and online operations. Formed with the vision of blending technical excellence with cooperative values, Navanirmithi has steadily grown into a trusted and dependable organization, catering to both private and public sector clients. Our society brings together a team of skilled professionals, qualified engineers, and experienced workers under one roof, capable of executing projects of varied scale and complexity. Whether it’s the construction of a residential building, a public infrastructure project, or a specialized service assignment, we ensure every task is completed with commitment, quality, and integrity.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading co-operative society in the construction and service industry, building a better future through trust, quality, and community development.

Mission: To deliver dependable, sustainable, and affordable services; promote employment and skill development; ensure quality, safety, and transparency in every project; and uphold cooperative values of teamwork and social progress.
